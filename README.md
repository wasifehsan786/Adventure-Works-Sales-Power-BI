# PowerBI - Adventure Works Sales Dashboard

## 📊 Case Study
This project presents a **Power BI dashboard** built on the Adventure Works dataset. The dashboard provides a comprehensive view of **sales performance, product categories, and order status**, enabling stakeholders to monitor revenue trends, customer activity, and product demand.

---

## Dataset
- **Data Source:** Adventure Works sample dataset  
- **Scope:** Orders, customers, product categories, and order totals across multiple months in 2023.  
- **Total Sales:** $173,700  

---

## Methodology
1. **Requirement Gathering**
   - Defined objectives: track order totals, product category performance, and order status distribution.
   - Focused on identifying top‑selling products and categories.

2. **Data Preparation**
   - Cleaned and structured order data.
   - Aggregated totals by product category, order date, and customer ID.

3. **Data Modeling**
   - Established relationships between orders, products, and customers.
   - Created measures for **Sum of Order Total, Quantity, and Product Weight**.

4. **Dashboard Development**
   - Visualizations:  
     - **Order totals by product category** (e.g., Road Bikes, Mountain Bikes, E‑Bikes).  
     - **Order totals by time (year, quarter, month, day)**.  
     - **Order status breakdown** (Shipped, Processing, Cancelled).  
   - Drill‑downs: product‑level analysis (e.g., XC‑Rider 2000, SpeedMaster 2000).  

---

## Key Insights
- **Top Categories:** Road Bikes, Mountain Bikes, and Touring Bikes drive the highest sales.  
- **Order Status:** Majority shipped successfully, but notable cancellations (e.g., Voyager 1000, SpeedMaster 1000).  
- **Monthly Trends:** Peaks in Feb–Apr 2023 with strong seasonal demand.  
- **High‑Value Orders:** SpeedMaster 2000 ($6,300) and GravityMaster 1000 ($8,800) stand out.
